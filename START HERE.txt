MOMOITTS - START HERE

1. Extract the entire ZIP into a writable folder and keep its files together.
2. Double-click Launch MomoiTTS.cmd. Runtime/dependencies are included.
3. Follow Finish setup to hear one character, test OBS with replies off,
   then create your paused Channel Points reward. See README.md for details.
4. Go live and click Turn replies on. Viewers redeem with Name: question.

Everyday use: Launch opens controls; turn replies on when ready.
Closing the browser tab leaves the app running. Exit MomoiTTS stops it.
Temporary disconnections recover fresh intake without replaying interrupted work.
Update MomoiTTS.cmd applies a downloaded release while preserving private data.

Saving settings never calls generation or speech. Explicit tests and normal
replies use account allowance and may incur charges for your selected models.
No credentials or accounts are included. Never share your .local folder.
If Twitch setup says the app registration is unavailable, ask the maintainer
for a configured release. Do not create your own Twitch app registration.
